# Early branding notes

The working product name is **Cue**: a short name that evokes a playlist cue,
a cue point, and choosing the next video without promising a particular source
or genre. “Cue — personal music-video library builder” is a useful descriptive
subtitle while the name gains context.

## Visual directions

1. **The cue card:** a slightly rounded video frame with a small play triangle
   and one offset card behind it. This is the cleanest app/icon direction and
   reads at small sizes.
2. **The crate:** a compact record/video crate with labeled dividers and a play
   symbol. It leans into the appealing “Video Crate” feeling without changing
   the product name, and can become an illustration motif in the dashboard.
3. **The viewing dog:** a warm cartoon dog watching a little CRT or monitor,
   with music notes escaping the screen. This is memorable for marketing or an
   empty state, but should complement—not replace—a simple small application
   icon.
4. **The cue beam:** a minimal spotlight/cone from a projector or screen,
   forming a subtle letter C around a play symbol. More modern and less literal.

My recommendation is a cue-card icon for the product/app mark, plus the
viewing dog as a friendly mascot for onboarding, empty states, and release art.
That provides both legibility at 16px and a distinct personality without making
the core interface feel toy-like.
